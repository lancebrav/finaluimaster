Our History

Barangay 663 has long been a cornerstone of the Ermita district in Manila. From its humble beginnings to becoming a digitally-integrated community, our barangay has remained committed to the core values of Filipino "Bayanihan." Barangays play a vital role in managing and maintaining records of residents within their jurisdiction. As the primary unit of governance, they are responsible for organizing demographic data, verifying residency, and providing essential documents such as Barangay Certificates, Certificates of Residency, and Certificates of Indigency.


Accurate and well-maintained resident records are crucial in ensuring efficient public service, proper identification, and effective implementation of community programs. The Barangay Hall of Barangay 663, originally located on Sylvia Street, was relocated to 663 Street, Ayala in 2017 to provide a more accessible and organized facility for both residents and barangay personnel.


Since its establishment, the barangay hall has served as the central office for managing resident information, processing document requests, and maintaining official records necessary for governance and public service delivery. As we move forward into 2026, we continue to evolve by adapting modern solutions—like this digital resident portal—to better serve our constituents, streamline document requests, and keep our community informed and safe.
